About Zyra Globex

Rooted in Tradition. Reaching the World.

Our Story

Zyra Globex was established with a clear vision: to connect carefully selected food products from Kerala with discerning buyers and markets worldwide.

We focus on coconut-based products, traditional sweeteners, tropical ingredients, and authentic regional foods — selected for their quality, consistency, and market suitability.

Our products are sourced through a trusted network of established production partners and undergo careful selection and quality evaluation before shipment. We place strong emphasis on product specifications, hygienic handling, appropriate packaging, traceability, and reliable export documentation.

From coconut oil and tender coconut to coconut jaggery, desiccated coconut, tamarind, and traditional snacks, our portfolio combines authentic regional character with international market expectations.

We are committed to building dependable, long-term partnerships through consistent products, responsive service, and reliable supply.
